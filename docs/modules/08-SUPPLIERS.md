# 0. Multi-Tenancy

- Suppliers, ledgers, payments, and documents carry `organization_id`.
- **Supplier phone is unique per organization**, not global.
- Supplier dues, ledgers, and history are per-organization; never shared across tenants.
- All supplier queries auto-scoped to the resolved tenant.

---

# 1. Purpose

The Suppliers module manages vendor relationships and procurement sources.

It integrates with:

- Purchases
- Inventory
- Finance
- Payments

Suppliers are the source of inventory entering the business.

---

# 2. Supplier Philosophy

Suppliers are long-term business partners.

The system must maintain:

- Purchase History
- Supplier Ledger
- Payment History
- Due History

Supplier records should never be hard deleted.

---

# 9. Supplier Due

Purpose:

Track outstanding payable balances.

---

## Formula

```text
Total Purchases

-

Total Payments

-

Purchase Returns

=

Supplier Due
```

---

## Rules

Supplier due cannot be negative.

# 30. API Responsibilities

Supplier APIs must:

- Validate supplier data
- Update supplier ledger
- Update dues
- Generate audit logs

---

## Supplier APIs Must Never

❌ Directly edit supplier due

❌ Delete ledger entries

❌ Delete purchase history

❌ Bypass services

---

# 31. Common Mistakes To Avoid

❌ Negative supplier due

❌ Deleting supplier records

❌ Deleting ledger entries

❌ Purchase without supplier

❌ Payment without transaction

❌ Returning more than received quantity

# 33. Golden Rules

Rule A

Suppliers are never hard deleted.

---

Rule B

Supplier due is calculated.

---

Rule C

Supplier ledger is immutable.

---

Rule D

Every supplier payment creates a transaction.

---

Rule E

Purchase orders require suppliers.

---

Rule F

Purchase returns affect supplier balances.

---

Rule G

Historical supplier data must remain intact.

---

Rule H

Supplier documents are stored in Cloudinary.

---

Rule I

Supplier payments must be auditable.

---

Rule J

Supplier relationships are long-term business assets.

---

Rule K

Suppliers are organization-scoped; phone is unique per organization.
